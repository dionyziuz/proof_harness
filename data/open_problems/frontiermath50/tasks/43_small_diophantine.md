# Large Integer Solutions to a Cubic Diophantine Equation

- **Problem ID**: `small-diophantine`
- **Field**: Diophantine
- **Notability**: Moderately interesting
- **Tags**: Integer Solution
- **Prompt Type**: `full_problem`
- **Status**: Open

---

## Problem Statement & Prompt

Find three distinct integer solutions (x, y, z) to the equation ___ such that: |x| > 10^50 and the value of x is different for each solution.

## Submission

Submit your three solutions using the submit tool as a list of three triples, each in the order [x, y, z]: [[x1, y1, z1], [x2, y2, z2], [x3, y3, z3]]. There is no upper bound on the magnitude of the coordinates. Your submission is checked exactly with arbitrary-precision integer arithmetic, so every coordinate must be an exact integer.
